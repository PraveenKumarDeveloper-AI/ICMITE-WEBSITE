About the College

CMS College of Engineering (Autonomous)

Established in 2007, CMS College of Engineering (Autonomous), Namakkal, is an AICTE-approved institution affiliated with Anna University, Chennai. The institution is committed to quality engineering education, innovation, research, and industry collaboration, with a focus on emerging technologies and sustainable development.

Conference Overview

About the Conference

A premier global platform uniting researchers, scientists, and engineers.

ICMITE 2027 provides an international platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to present innovative research and exchange knowledge.

The conference focuses on multidisciplinary research, emerging technologies, innovation, sustainability, and industry-oriented solutions addressing contemporary global challenges.

Publication Opportunities

Selected high-quality papers will be considered for peer-reviewed journal indexing.

Scopus Indexed Journals Web of Science (WoS) Peer-Reviewed

Journal Consideration & Dissemination

Selected high-quality papers will be considered for publication in Scopus / Web of Science indexed journals, subject to peer review, editorial approval, journal scope, and applicable Article Processing Charges (APC).

Peer Review

Rigorous technical evaluation by designated domain specialists and academic referees.

Editorial Approval

Final acceptance governed strictly by respective journal editors and academic boards.

Journal Scope

Research manuscripts must closely match the thematic aims and focus of the target journal.

Applicable APC

Article Processing Charges (APC), where applicable, are determined by the respective publishers.

Publication Policy Notice: Publication in Scopus / Web of Science indexed journals is subject to selection, independent peer review, editorial approval, journal scope, and applicable APC. Publication is not automatic or guaranteed for all presented papers.
